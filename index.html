Google Ads playbook

Eight campaign types, one journey

Scroll to move through each campaign type. The text explains what it does, and the illustration on the right shows how it works.

01 / 08

Search campaigns

Show up at the moment someone searches for what you sell. Keywords are grouped by theme, bids are set per ad group, and every click lands on the most relevant page.

  • Themed ad groups and keyword sets
  • Ad-group target bids
  • Location and audience targeting
Best forHigh-intent leads and sales
02 / 08

Performance Max

One campaign serves ads across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps, and moves budget toward the placements that perform best.

  • Audience signals to guide automation
  • ROAS-focused bidding
  • Asset groups by product theme
Best forE-commerce revenue
03 / 08

Display campaigns

Image and responsive ads shown on websites and apps your audience already visits. They keep a brand visible between searches.

  • Audience and placement targeting
  • Responsive image ads
  • Placement reviews and exclusions
Best forAwareness and reach
04 / 08

Demand Gen

Visual ads that sit inside YouTube, Discover and Gmail feeds. Lookalike segments reach new people who resemble your best customers.

  • Image and video creatives
  • Lookalike segments
  • Feed-native placements
Best forCreating new demand
05 / 08

Display and Demand Gen together

Display builds broad awareness and Demand Gen turns that interest into consideration. Run together, they cover the top and middle of the funnel and fill remarketing lists.

  • One shared audience strategy
  • Awareness through to consideration
  • Feeds warm audiences to Search
Best forFull-funnel growth
06 / 08

Retargeting

Most visitors leave without buying. Retargeting shows them a reminder elsewhere on the web and brings them back when they are ready to decide.

  • Past visitor and customer audiences
  • Behavior insights from GA and Clarity
  • Messaging matched to the page they left
Best forRecovering lost visitors
07 / 08

Dynamic Search Ads

Google reads the website and writes headlines from page content, catching searches a keyword list would miss.

  • Page feeds and URL targeting
  • Auto-generated headlines
  • Finding keyword gaps
Best forLarge product catalogues
08 / 08

AI Max for Search

Google matches relevant searches beyond the keyword list. Location and audience signals keep that reach useful, and search terms that will never convert are excluded.

  • Search term review and exclusions
  • Location and audience signals
  • Bulk edits in Google Ads Editor
Best forScaling Search reach
